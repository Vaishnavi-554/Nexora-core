HIGHWAY CRASH SAFETY CHALLENGE

THE PROBLEM WITH RIGID DIVIDERS

Traditional concrete medians and steel W-beams stop vehicles abruptly with catastrophic kinetic shock, often remaining unmonitored for long periods.

PASSIVE & RIGID

Traditional Rigid Divider

Rigid concrete barriers or stiff steel guardrails that do not dissipate vehicle momentum.

Vehicle Collision ➔ Rigid Obstacle ➔ Sudden Deceleration & Crushing Impact
  • ❌ High Impact Shockwave: Severe deceleration causes internal injuries and fatal trauma.
  • ❌ Extreme Vehicle Damage: High probability of rollover, roof crush, or rebounding into oncoming traffic.
  • ❌ Infrastructure Crumble: Expensive concrete cracking requiring prolonged highway lane closures.
  • ❌ Unnoticed Remote Crashes: Nighttime or blind-curve accidents remain unreported for 20–40 minutes.
  • ❌ Golden Hour Delay: Critical medical treatment is delayed due to vague caller locations.
INTELLIGENT & ROTATIONAL

Smart Rolling Barrier (NEXORA)

Engineered cylindrical rollers converting crash energy into safe rotational deflection with edge AIoT monitoring.

Vehicle Collision ➔ Rotating Cylinders ➔ Energy Conversion ➔ IoT Telemetry ➔ Emergency Dispatch
  • ✅ Rotational Energy Absorption: Cylinders spin on impact, gradually decelerating the vehicle safely.
  • ✅ Deflection Control: Re-guides colliding vehicles back along the road direction, preventing rollovers.
  • ✅ Instant Millisecond Detection: Accelerometer + Load Cell + Tilt sensors detect collision instantly.
  • ✅ Sub-3-Second Alerts: Automated emergency API dispatches to Police, Hospital, and Ambulances.
  • ✅ Precise GIS Coordinates: Pinpoints the exact barrier ID and highway kilometer marker.
